WHY THIS RUN EXISTS. The 5-band confirmation ran n=15 per band. `embeddings`
pointed out that 2000+ is the band where the prefix could plausibly HURT — bare
qwen3 already BEATS 3-large there (42.4% vs 18.8%) — and that n=15 cannot support
"no harm" when you are looking for a decrease against your strongest baseline.
Correct, so the band was re-run at n=120, seed 101, paired, depth 10.

SAMPLE IS REPRESENTATIVE: bare rank-1 here is 54/120 = 45.0%, against the full
census's 42.4% on the same band. The draw is not flattering itself.

misses" and "2 documents you can no longer find" are both true at once, and the
net prices them identically.

⚠️ MULTIPLICITY — three metrics on one dataset. A p=0.055 among three tests is
roughly what noise produces at least once, so the two marginal rows carry less
than their face value. The decisive point is which metric was named FIRST:

  PRIMARY   rank-1 discordant pairs. Pre-registered before the run, in this
            script's docstring and in the collection instruction: "worsened == 0
            is the only thing that supports 'prefix everywhere'." It is NULL
            (p=0.202) and worsened is 9.
  SECONDARY top-5 (p=0.073) and absent (p=0.055).

⛔ A MARGINAL SECONDARY DOES NOT RESCUE A NULL PRIMARY. Reading it as though it
did is the standard route by which an equivocal result becomes a positive one,
and it would be especially available here because the secondaries both point the
flattering way. (Raised by `embeddings`; it hardens the reading rather than
softening it.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

✅ ESTABLISHED, and it was the point of the run: n=15 was not enough. The n=15 look
   at this band showed +2 rank-1 with ZERO worsened. At n=120 there are NINE
   worsened. "No harm" at n=15 was not a finding, it was an absence of resolution.

⛔ NOT ESTABLISHED: that the prefix helps in this band. p=0.20.
⛔ NOT ESTABLISHED: that it is safe here. Nine demotions and two documents lost.
⛔ NOT ESTABLISHED: band-dependent routing. Two seats (memo, groton) independently
   failed to resolve individual bands; that reproducibility makes it a property of
   the effect size, not of one sample. There is no basis to route by length, so the
   choice is uniform-or-nothing WITHIN this corpus.

⚠️ THIS IS NOT THE NEGATIVE RESULT I PRE-REGISTERED AGAINST. I said a negative
   2000+ would mean "don't apply it anywhere yet". This is not negative — it is a
   NULL on rank-1 with a marginal favourable absent column and a real, enumerable
   set of harmed documents. Saying "the prefix hurts long documents" would overstate
   it in the opposite direction to my original error. It does not help them, and it
   moves nine of them the wrong way.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROPORTION, so this band is not read as the whole decision
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 2000+ band is 399 of 7,221 documents — 5.5% of the corpus. The three short
bands, where the effect is strong and significant, hold 5,516 documents — 76%.
Uniform application is expected to be a large net win whose cost is concentrated
here. That trade is Ben's to make, not mine to pre-empt, and it should be made
with the nine titles above visible rather than as a summary statistic.

⛔ REGARDLESS: nothing ships until embed_query()/embed_document() exist and ~21
call sites are reclassified by hand. memo has no chokepoint — see the commit
message on 2369889.

SCOPE. Measures THE TASK STRING BELOW, not "prefixing" in general. `groton`
measured 5/10 generic vs 7/10 domain-specific on their corpus, the better one with
the worse tail — the string is a live variable.
  "Instruct: Given a search query, retrieve relevant documents that match the
   query\nQuery: {q}"
Self-retrieval by title makes the correct answer true by construction; read `bare`
before the delta. Bare is 45.0% here, so the design has room and a null is readable.

⚠️ The run executed a build of the script that predates these two scope notes by
~30 seconds; they are recorded here rather than in the run's stdout. The numbers
are unaffected. Stated because a file that implies a caveat was printed when it
was not is worse than one carrying no caveat.
