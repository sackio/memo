sampled. That column carries the cross-seat conclusion that memo's prefix RECALLS

two draws). I had one draw when I made that claim and flagged it as mine to
retract. It does not need retracting: -40% twice against ~-3% twice is an order of
magnitude apart and now reproducible on both sides.

⇒ Fusion absorbs a reorder. It does not absorb a changed candidate set. The same
change is therefore worth materially more at memo than at groton, and neither
seat's number predicts the other's.
